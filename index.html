Choosing Zippers for a Custom Bag

Zippers are small, but they are the part of a bag people touch most. These notes cover the choices worth settling early in development.

  1. Coil, moulded or metal teeth. Coil zips are flexible and suit curved openings; moulded plastic teeth feel sporty; metal teeth look classic but add weight.
  2. Size. Larger gauges suit main compartments and heavy use; smaller gauges suit pockets and pouches.
  3. Puller style. A branded puller or a fabric pull tab is an easy way to add identity without extra logos.
  4. Two-way or one-way. Two sliders let a main compartment open from either side; one slider is simpler for pockets.
  5. Water-resistant zips. Coated zips help outdoor and travel bags, but they run a little stiffer.
  6. Colour. Matching tapes look tidy; contrast tapes make the opening a design feature.

Tip: write the zip type and size for every opening on your spec sheet so each sample round stays consistent.
